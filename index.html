APERTURE

PORTAL PHYSICS PLAYGROUND

No levels, no rules. Portals, physics, and a lot of things to throw.
Press Esc any time to pause.

HOW TO PLAY

SPEEDY THING GOES IN, SPEEDY THING COMES OUT

  1. Shoot a blue and an orange portal onto white surfaces. Anything entering one exits the other with its speed.
  2. Pick a prop with 1-0 or the scroll wheel, spawn it with F, then grab (E) and throw it (click) through portals.
  3. Jump off the central tower into a floor portal and exit an angled panel for huge flings.
  4. Blue gel bounces you, orange gel makes you fast, Faith Plates launch you, red barrels explode.
  5. Shoot hoops on the basketball court. A shot that passes through a portal first is worth 3.
  6. Fly through the floating orange rings to collect them all. Stay out of the green goo.
